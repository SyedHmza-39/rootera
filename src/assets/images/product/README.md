# ROOTERA product images

Save your images in THIS folder using these exact file names.
The extension can be .png, .jpg, .jpeg, .webp or .avif.

| File name (no extension) | What it is |
|---|---|
| bottle-white      | 1. Single bottle on a clean white background |
| bottle-beige      | 2. Single bottle on a premium beige background |
| bottle-closeup    | 3. Close-up product shot |
| bottle-botanical  | 4. Bottle with subtle botanical elements |
| bottle-lifestyle  | 5. Premium lifestyle product shot |
| bottle-cutout     | Optional: bottle on a transparent background (used in the hero) |

Recommended: square (1:1), 1600 x 1600 px, under 500 KB each (use WebP or compressed JPG).

## How to generate them

Use an image tool that can accept a reference photo (for example ChatGPT image
editing, Gemini, or Photoshop Generative Fill). Attach your original ROOTERA photo
with EVERY prompt below.

IMPORTANT: AI tools often garble small text. Always zoom in on the label and
check that every word is spelled correctly. If the text is wrong, regenerate,
or fix the label in an editor. Never publish an image with misspelled label text.

### Identity lock (paste at the start of every prompt)

Use the attached photo as the exact reference for the product. Show ONLY the
bottle, with no cardboard box. The bottle is a frosted glass dropper bottle with a
black rubber bulb and black collar, and a white wrap-around label. Keep the label
exactly as in the reference: gold "ROOTERA" wordmark, "Advanced Hair Growth
Serum", gold italic "Root Activation + Density Boost", "With Caffeine + Biotin +
Rosemary", "Non-Greasy | Lightweight | Unisex", and "Net Volume 100 ml". Do not
change the spelling, fonts, layout, or colors of the label. Do not add any other
brand, logo, or text.

### 1. bottle-white
[Identity lock] Single bottle, front-facing, centered, filling about 80% of the
frame height, on a pure clean white seamless background. Soft even studio
lighting, a very subtle natural contact shadow and faint reflection under the
bottle. High-end e-commerce product photography, sharp focus, square 1:1 format.

### 2. bottle-beige
[Identity lock] Single bottle, front-facing, centered, on a soft warm beige
seamless background (color close to #F2EDE4). Soft diffused window light, gentle
shadow to one side, premium minimalist skincare photography, square 1:1 format.

### 3. bottle-closeup
[Identity lock] Close-up macro shot of the upper half of the bottle: the black
dropper collar, the frosted glass shoulder, and the top of the label with the gold
ROOTERA wordmark. Shallow depth of field, soft side lighting, neutral warm-white
background. Label text must be sharp and fully readable. Square 1:1 format.

### 4. bottle-botanical
[Identity lock] Single bottle standing on a warm-white stone surface with a few
fresh rosemary sprigs placed beside the base. Restrained and minimal, with no
clutter. Do not let any leaves cover the label. Soft natural light, beige-toned
background, premium editorial look, square 1:1 format.

### 5. bottle-lifestyle
[Identity lock] Single bottle on a minimal light-stone or pale wood bathroom
shelf, with a folded white towel softly blurred in the background. Calm morning
light, neutral warm tones, shallow depth of field. No people, no other products.
Premium editorial lifestyle photography, square 1:1 format.

### Optional: bottle-cutout
Cut the bottle out of a high-resolution version of image 1 (remove.bg, Photoshop
"Remove Background", or similar) and export as a transparent PNG.

## Negative instructions (add to every prompt)
No cardboard box, no extra bottles, no extra text, no watermark, no distorted or
misspelled label text, no people.
